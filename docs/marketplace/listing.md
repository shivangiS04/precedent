## Short description (≤ 150 characters)
Record what your team decided and why. Precedent flags new Jira work that conflicts with a past decision and tells you who to ask.

## Full description
Teams make decisions in Confluence pages, Jira comments and meetings, then forget them. Months later someone starts work that contradicts a decision, or the team argues it all over again.

Precedent keeps a structured **Decision Ledger** with the decision, its rationale, the alternatives it rejected, its owner, its status and its source page or work item.

- **Conflict checks in Jira:** when work is created or changed, Precedent compares it with live decisions. The work item panel shows conflicts and related decisions, with the rejected option each one collides with.
- **Record as decision:** on a Confluence page, pre-fill a decision from an ADR-style page.
- **Ask Precedent in Rovo:** ask "what did we decide about caching and why?" or "does this ticket conflict with anything?"
- **Who to ask:** owners, deciders, and related teams and goals (Teamwork Graph, when your admin has it on).
- **Revisit reminders:** owners are told when a decision reaches its revisit date.
- **Admin controls:** turn labels, comments and graph context on or off; add keyword aliases.

Precedent only shows decisions a person is allowed to see: a decision is hidden unless its sources are visible to that person.

## Highlights (for the listing)
1. Explainable matches: "Matches Redis, which this decision rejected".
2. Decisions are only shown to people who can see their sources.
3. Works without AI: matching is deterministic. An optional AI re-rank is planned, not enabled.
