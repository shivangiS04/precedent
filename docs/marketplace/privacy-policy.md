# Precedent privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

## What Precedent does
Precedent lets your team record decisions, shows when new Jira work conflicts with a recorded decision, and helps people find who to talk to.

## Information Precedent stores
- **Decisions:** title, statement, rationale, status, dates, rejected alternatives, keywords, and project and component names that your team enters.
- **Account IDs:** the Atlassian account IDs of decision owners, deciders, and people who mark a conflict as not related or propose a revisit. Account IDs are the only personal data stored.
- **Links:** Jira issue keys and Confluence page IDs with their URLs, so decisions point to their sources.
- **Conflict results:** a short cache of matched decision IDs and confidence, so badges can load quickly.
- **Settings:** admin choices such as keyword aliases.

Precedent does not copy Jira issue descriptions, comments, or Confluence page bodies into its own storage. It reads them when a check runs, to compute matches.

## Who can see what
Precedent shows a decision only to people who can view all of its sources in Jira and Confluence. Access follows Atlassian's own permissions.

## Where data is stored
Precedent stores its data in Forge SQL and Forge storage, hosted by Atlassian.

## Who Precedent talks to
Precedent only calls your own site's Jira, Confluence and Teamwork Graph APIs. It sends no data to other services.

## Your choices
- Admins can switch off labels, comments, and Teamwork Graph context in Precedent settings.
- Anyone can mark a conflict as not related, or restore it.
- To have your data deleted, contact the support address on the app listing.
